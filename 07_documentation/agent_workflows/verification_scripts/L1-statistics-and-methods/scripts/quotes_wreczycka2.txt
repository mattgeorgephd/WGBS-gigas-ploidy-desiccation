We believe that a good compromise between the DSS/limma/F-test and default methylKit test is the overdispersion corrected methylKit Chi-square test.
A good alternative to stringent and relaxed methods is Chi-square test after overdispersion correction (implemented in methylKit).
This method has high sensitivity without sacrificing too much for specificity.
the Chi-squared test with overdispersion correction has similar specificity to stringent methods such as DSS and limma but achieves higher sensitivity
we defined differentially methylated CpG sites with q-values below 0.01 for all examined methods
One can choose methods based on the overall goal of their research.
