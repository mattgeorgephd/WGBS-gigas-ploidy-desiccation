# 05_manuscript

The manuscript's figures, table and supplementary lists under the numbers the manuscript uses, and the table that ties each number quoted in the manuscript to the pipeline output that regenerates it. Everything here is written by `01_pipeline/13_manuscript_values.R` from the outputs in `04_output/`; nothing is drawn, typed or edited by hand. Rerun step 13 after any step that changes an output.

## Contents

| Path | Contents |
|---|---|
| `manuscript_values.csv` | One row per quantitative statement of the draft: section, the clause it comes from (`quote`), the manuscript value, the value the pipeline regenerates with the draft's own analysis (`regenerated_legacy`), a status, the value in corrected mode (`regenerated_corrected`: the draft's model with the upper coverage filter that the Methods describe, I-05, and for GO the gene-level analysis of step 10) and whether that differs, the issues of [`issues.md`](../issues.md) that concern it, a note, and the R expressions that computed both values |
| `manuscript_values_summary.csv` | Counts of statements by section and status |
| `figures/Figure_1.*` | Figure 1 (does not depend on the mode) |
| `figures/legacy/` | Figures 2 to 9 and S1 as the draft's analysis gives them, regenerated in R. Figures 5 to 9 reproduce analyses that the audit found unsound (issues I-03, I-20, I-21, I-39 to I-41, I-48); they are reproductions, not results |
| `figures/corrected/` | Figures 2 to 4 and S1 with the coverage filter that the Methods describe (issue I-05), and two figures of the gene-level enrichment |
| `tables/legacy/Table_1.csv` | Table 1 as in the draft |
| `supplement/legacy/`, `supplement/corrected/` | The lists the draft's section 7 names: DML of each set, genes holding DML, and (legacy only) the GO terms with p < 0.01 of the draft's test and the DML in genes that carry one of its ten top-ranked terms per ontology (the draft's "genes with enriched GO terms that contain DML"; issue I-21) |
| `supplement/GO_gene_level_summary.csv`, `supplement/GO_terms_of_draft_retested.csv` | The gene-level enrichment in summary, and each of the draft's ten top-ranked terms retested at gene level |

PNG and PDF versions of each figure are written. PDF files carry a time stamp and show as changed in git after a rerun even when the content is the same.

## Reading `manuscript_values.csv`

- `status` is `matches` when the regenerated value equals the manuscript value within the tolerance of the row, `differs` when it does not, and `not regenerated` when the statement is about something the pipeline does not compute (a laboratory detail, a design statement, a software version); the note says why.
- A row can match and still be wrong science. "Matches" means the pipeline reproduces the number, not that the number means what the text says. The `issue` column points to the entry of `issues.md` that covers it, and `corrected_differs` marks the rows whose value changes in corrected mode. Corrected mode is not the analysis that `issues.md` supports for the DML themselves (I-06) or for their location (I-41).
- State on 2026-11-19: 545 statements (Methods, Results 3.1 to 3.5, Table 1, figure legends, Discussion and Conclusions). 342 match, 146 differ, and 57 cannot be regenerated by a pipeline (laboratory and design details); for 158 the second expression gives a value that does not agree with the draft. Counts by section are in `manuscript_values_summary.csv`. The statements that differ are concentrated in Results 3.4 (34 of 44) and the Discussion (60 of 115): most are statements that a function is enriched, that a difference exists at baseline, or that a response to heat differs by ploidy (issues I-41, I-48, I-02, I-03, I-01, I-07).
